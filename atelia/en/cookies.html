Cookie and local storage policy

Version 1.1 · effective 27 November 2026 · under Romanian Law 506/2004 (ePrivacy) and the GDPR · English translation — if the versions differ, the Romanian version prevails.

In short: Atelia Build uses no analytics or advertising cookies. We only use your browser's local storage (localStorage), which is strictly necessary for the App to work. The law does not require consent for this, but we inform you about it.

Offline cache (strictly necessary): your browser keeps a copy of the App's files (pages, 3D/PDF libraries) so Atelia Build opens quickly and without internet. It contains no data about you and updates automatically.

Optional categories

Analytics and Marketing: we currently use neither. If we introduce them, they will be off by default and only start if you explicitly accept them in "Cookie settings", where refusing will be as easy as accepting. The list above will be updated beforehand.

How to change your choice

In the App: tap the logo → "Cookie settings". You can delete all data at any time in your browser settings (Clear site data). Note: this also deletes projects saved locally.
