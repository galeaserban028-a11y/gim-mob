Subscriptions, cancellation and refunds

Version 1.0 · effective when paid subscriptions launch · part of the Terms and conditions · English translation — if the versions differ, the Romanian version prevails.

In short: try it free for 7 days, no card needed. The subscription is billed monthly, can be cancelled at any time from your account and does not renew from the next month. Consumers have a 14-day right of withdrawal under the conditions below.

The final price including VAT is shown before payment. Payment is by card through the Stripe payment processor; we never see or store your card details.

2. Automatic renewal

The subscription renews automatically every month on the same date until you cancel. We email you before any price change, and a new price only applies if you accept it.

3. Cancellation

You can cancel at any time from "My profile" → "Subscription", with no reason and no penalty. Access stays active until the end of the paid month; no further payment is taken. Your projects stay saved and you can export them at any time.

4. Right of withdrawal (consumers)

If you are a consumer, you may withdraw from the contract within 14 days of concluding it, without giving a reason. If you explicitly ask to start using the paid service immediately within those 14 days, we will ask for your express consent and your acknowledgement that you lose the right of withdrawal once the service has been fully provided; an amount proportional to the period already used may be retained. To withdraw, write to [CONTACT EMAIL].

5. Refunds

  • Withdrawal within the legal period: refunded within 14 days, to the same card.
  • Wrong or double payment, or a significant outage caused by us: we refund the corresponding amount.
  • Otherwise, months already started are not refunded, but the subscription stays active until the end of the month.

6. Businesses

The consumer right of withdrawal does not apply to business customers; the other rules (cancel any time, access until the end of the month) still apply. Invoices are issued to the company details in the account.
