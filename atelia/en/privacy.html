3. Technical providers

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.): hosts the App.
  • Supabase (project hosted in the EU): online accounts and projects — only if you create an account.
  • Cloudflare Workers AI: interpreting descriptions in "Generate" — only when the AI feature is active. Without it, descriptions are interpreted locally in your browser.

These providers technically receive your device's IP address when the page loads. Some may process data outside the European Economic Area under the transfer mechanisms provided by the GDPR. The App's libraries (3D, PDF, QR codes) and fonts are hosted on the App's own server, with no requests to other servers.

4. How long we keep data

Data in your browser stays until you delete it (in browser settings or by deleting projects in the App). We keep no copies.

"My profile" → "Delete account and all online data" permanently deletes your account, profile and online projects. Browser data is deleted from your browser settings.
